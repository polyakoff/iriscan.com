The Manifest Highlights Iriscan as Ukraine’s Most Recommended Cybersecurity Company

In October 2021 The Manifest, a business directory from the team behind Clutch, listed Iriscan among the 15 most-reviewed cybersecurity companies in Ukraine. The ranking counts verified reviews from clients, and ours mentioned pricing, project management and the quality of the work.

Iriscan is registered in Estonia as Biometric Technologies OÜ and works with clients internationally. Part of our engineering team is based in Kyiv, which is why directories such as Clutch and The Manifest list us among Ukrainian companies.

Thank you to the clients who took the time to write those reviews.

Since then our focus has narrowed to biometric identity. We build open-source components (a face, liveness and iris SDK for mobile and server, a fingerprint template converter) and the services around them: an iris recognition API, offline enrolment stations, integration and support for national ID, humanitarian and fintech projects. Our face match SDK is used in Inji Wallet, the reference wallet of the MOSIP identity platform.

Working on identity verification?

Tell us about the project: who is being identified, how many people, on what devices. We will tell you honestly what we can do and what it would take.

For a small team there is a $990 starter kit with two scanners, three months of cloud enrollment and setup.
