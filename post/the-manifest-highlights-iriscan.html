The Manifest Highlights Iriscan as Ukraine’s Most Recommended Cybersecurity Company

Why is it that security is sometimes not part of the main agenda for businesses? While it’s the most important variable next to sales. We at Iriscan understand how tough it is to run a company, and that sometimes, unfortunately, leads to missing some key points. That’s why we started this business. We aim to make an impact on our clients through our cybersecurity efforts.

Because of the extra dedication we put into our clients’ projects, we’ve been highlighted by The Manifest as Ukraine’s most recommended cybersecurity company for 2021! We are humbled by this wonderful news.

When we started the company back in 2015, we understood the demand and importance of cybersecurity. Our team worked tirelessly to make sure our clients feel safe and are at ease with us by their side. Since the beginning, client satisfaction has meant a lot to us. That’s why when we found out about this recognition, we were beyond excited!

For those who don’t know, The Manifest is an informational guide and business blog platform that encompasses the IT, marketing, and business services spaces. The site is known for its commitment to providing helpful industry wisdom through survey reports, agency rankings, and how-to articles.

According to their most recent findings, Iriscan ranks among the top 15 firms in the category that received the most reviews and praise from clients throughout the years. Our clients greatly emphasized our pricing, project management, quality, and their satisfaction, hence, we were chosen for this amazing recognition.

We want to send our thanks and appreciation to everyone who helped make this a reality. It’s been six fantastic years of learnings, new relationships, and bright horizons for us at Iriscan. We are looking forward to welcoming more opportunities in the future.

Got a project in mind? Let’s make sure that your networks are impenetrable! Contact the Iriscan team and let’s get to work. We are excited to get to know you even more.
