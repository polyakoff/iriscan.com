Pilots and projects

For insurers, national schemes, lenders with large agent networks and public institutions we run a pilot first: a few weeks on your own population, hardware and systems, with success criteria agreed in advance. After that the system runs in our cloud or on your servers. See solutions for examples.

Questions

Who owns the enrolled data?

You do. Iris and face images are stored in ISO formats, and you can export them at any time.

Can we move to our own servers later?

Yes. The same matcher runs on premises when regulation or volume requires it.

Which scanners work?

The scanners we supply, and most off-the-shelf iris scanners that produce standard images. Ask us about the model you already have.

Do prices include VAT?

No. Prices exclude VAT, and delivery of devices is quoted for your country.

Not sure which option fits?

Tell us how many people you serve, where you meet them and how you identify them today. We will recommend a kit, a plan or a pilot.
