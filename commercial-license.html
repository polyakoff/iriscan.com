Ship our open-source SDK inside a closed-source product

The Iriscan Biometric SDK and the biometric data converter are free under GPL-3.0. If you build them into an app or a device that you distribute without publishing your own source code, a commercial licence gives you the right to do that.

Request a licence   SDK on GitHub

GPL or commercial: which one you need

What you do with the SDKLicence
Research, evaluation, tests, internal toolsGPL-3.0, free
Run it on your own servers, for example behind an API, without handing the software to anyoneGPL-3.0, free
Build it into an open-source product released under GPL-3.0GPL-3.0, free
Ship it inside a closed-source Android or iOS app, desktop software or device firmwareCommercial licence
Deliver it to a client as part of a system whose source code they will not receiveCommercial licence

The code is the same in both cases. A commercial licence changes the terms you get it under, not the software, so you can still read and audit every line on GitHub.

What is covered

  • Biometric SDK. Kotlin Multiplatform for Android, iOS and the JVM: iris segmentation, quality, encoding and matching, face matching and liveness, ISO/IEC 19794 records.
  • Biometric data converter. Conversion between fingerprint and iris template formats.

Our de-duplication server is licensed separately for installation on your own servers; see On your servers on the pricing page.

What you get

  • The right to embed and distribute the SDK in proprietary products without the GPL-3.0 obligations.
  • Updates released during the licence term.
  • Help with integration from the engineers who wrote the code.

Price

Licences are annual and priced per product, by platform and scale of deployment. Tell us what you are building and how many devices or users it will reach, and we will send you an offer.
