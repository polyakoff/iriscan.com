How it works

  1. Get scanners. USB iris scanners from €139 connect to the laptop or Android tablet you already use.
  2. Enroll on our cloud. Each person is scanned once and checked against everyone already enrolled, so nobody is registered twice.
  3. Identify in seconds. Next time the person looks at the scanner and you get their record, without a card or a document.
