Services

Consulting and integration for biometric projects

We help you choose the modality and the hardware, connect identification to your systems and keep biometric data compliant. The same team builds our iris cloud and the open-source Biometric SDK.

What we do

Biometric consulting

Which modality fits your population, which scanners work in your conditions, where to set the matching threshold and how to design a pilot that proves the business case.

Integration

Our cloud or SDK, or another vendor's matcher, connected to your CRM, core banking, hospital information system or MOSIP, including offline agents that synchronize later.

Custom development

Mobile enrollment apps, back-office tools and secure, high-load systems that process sensitive data.

Data protection

Legal basis, consent, encryption, separation of biometric data from personal records and retention rules under GDPR and local law.

How we work

  1. A short call. You tell us what you need to identify, where and for whom.
  2. A written proposal. Fixed scope, timeline and price, or a pilot with success criteria.
  3. Delivery. We build, integrate and test with your team, on your data and hardware.
  4. Support. We stay available after launch and hand over everything you need to run it yourself.
